Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 6, 2026

T&S Policy Watcher is committed to being usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, voice control or other assistive technology.

Standard we follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. These guidelines are the standard generally used to judge website accessibility under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and similar laws.

What we have done

  • The dashboard's tabs, tables, cards, filters, pop-up windows and subscribe form can be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link. Wide tables can be scrolled with the arrow keys.
  • Buttons, links, form fields and menus have names that screen readers announce, and icons used only for decoration are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Pop-up windows (policy summaries, version history and week details) behave as dialogs: focus moves into them and stays inside while they are open, and closing with the Close button, the Escape key or a click outside returns focus to the control that opened them. Closing the subscribe panel returns focus to its button.
  • Status messages, such as a confirmed subscription or an email that needs fixing, are announced to screen readers.
  • Data tables have captions and column headers, and the weekly change chart has a text description and an equivalent data table.
  • The page uses headings and landmarks so it can be navigated by section, respects the "reduce motion" setting and can be zoomed to 200% or viewed on a narrow screen without losing content.
  • Testing in November 2026: automated checks with axe-core on every tab, pop-up window and the subscribe form at desktop and 320px widths, plus keyboard-only walkthroughs in Chromium. An independent review found two pop-up focus problems, which were fixed and retested on November 6, 2026. We have not yet completed a full pass with a native screen reader such as VoiceOver or NVDA.

Known limitations

  • Policy summaries are written automatically by an AI model from the source policy pages. They may contain complex wording, and their structure depends on the source text.
  • Links to TikTok, Meta, YouTube, Whatnot and Twitch policy pages lead to third-party websites that we do not control. Their accessibility is the responsibility of those companies.

If any part of this site is hard for you to use, we want to hear about it and will provide the information in another format on request.

Feedback and contact

To report an accessibility problem or ask for information in another format, email hello@lyori.com. No account is needed. If you prefer, you can also open an accessibility issue on GitHub (needs a GitHub account). Please tell us the page, what you were trying to do and the browser or assistive technology you were using. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
