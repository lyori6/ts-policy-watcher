How Whatnot approaches enforcement
Most people don’t come to Whatnot to break the rules. That’s why, whenever we can, we explain what happened and give you the opportunity to fix it.
Our approach is:
Proportional:
Penalties match the severity of the violation and your account history.
Cumulative:
Repeated violations—even minor ones—can lead to stronger penalties over time.
Fair:
If we take action on your account, you can review it and appeal if you think we got it wrong.
What happens if I violate the Community Guidelines?
Depending on the severity of the violation and your account history, you may receive:
Warning:
You receive a notice explaining the issue and the relevant policy, so you can avoid future violations. For example, smoking or vaping around the products that you’re selling may result in a warning.
Feature restrictions:
You temporarily or permanently lose access to certain features, like Direct Messages or Giveaways. For example, repeated unfulfilled giveaways may cause you to lose access to the Giveaways feature.
Discovery restricted:
If you’re a seller, your visibility in Feeds and Recommendations is temporarily reduced. Your shows will still appear on your profile, in Search, and in the “Followed Hosts” feed. For example, a show title or listing that doesn’t follow our guidelines may result in discovery restrictions for your account.
Temporary account suspension (2 or 7 days):
You lose access to your account for a set period. When the suspension ends, your access is restored unless you’re notified otherwise. For example, breaking the same rule again after a warning may result in an account suspension.
Ongoing investigation suspension:
In certain cases, your account is suspended while a serious issue is under review. The suspension stays in place until the review is complete. For example, if we receive multiple claims of selling counterfeit items, your account may be suspended for the duration of the review.
Selling suspension:
If you’re a seller, you temporarily lose the ability to sell or host shows. You can still access your Seller Hub, ship orders, respond to support requests, and use DMs during the suspension period. Your Account Health rates continue to update based on how you handle existing orders and buyer requests during the suspension. Once it ends, your ability to sell and host shows will be restored unless you’re notified otherwise. If you are repeatedly shipping late, your selling may be suspended while you catch up on open orders.
Loss of selling access:
If you’re a seller, you permanently lose the ability to sell or host shows on Whatnot. Once you lose selling access, you cannot access any seller features. For example, evading enforcement action from Whatnot will lead to the loss of selling access.
Ban:
Your account is permanently banned. You can no longer buy or sell on Whatnot. Repeated violations, or a single instance of a severe violation, such as manipulating the outcome of a Card Break, will result in your account being banned.
To maintain the integrity of our marketplace, you can’t use a different account or other workarounds to use Whatnot if your account has been banned, suspended, or restricted. This is against our
Community Guidelines
, and action may also extend to related accounts.
Do violations expire?
Most violations expire after
180 days.
Permanent penalties, like
bans or loss of selling access, don’t expire
.
How do I appeal a violation?
If you believe a penalty was applied in error, you can appeal by logging in to Whatnot and going to the
Policy Standing
section of your
Account Health dashboard
. There, you can review the violation and submit an appeal if you’re eligible.
After you submit an appeal, our team will review the relevant details and notify you of the outcome.
How can I check my account standing?
Visit the
Account Activity
section in your
Account Health dashboard
to view your overall standing and any violations from the past 180 days.
Checking this regularly can help you stay in good standing, understand any issues on your account, and avoid future penalties.
