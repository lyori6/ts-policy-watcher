At Whatnot, we’re building a marketplace that brings millions of people together to shop, sell, and connect around the things they love.
To help keep Whatnot safe and fair for everyone, we take action when accounts violate our
Community Guidelines
and
Terms of Service
.
How Whatnot approaches enforcement
Our goal is simple: maintain a trusted marketplace while giving people the opportunity to learn and correct mistakes whenever possible.
Our approach is:
Proportional:
Penalties match the severity of the violation and your account history.
Cumulative:
Repeated violations—even less severe ones—can lead to stronger penalties over time.
Fair:
If we take enforcement action on your account, you can review it and appeal if you think we got it wrong.
What happens if I violate the Community Guidelines?
Depending on the severity of the violation and your account history, you may receive:
Warning:
You receive a notice explaining the issue and the relevant policy, so you can avoid future violations.
Feature restrictions:
You temporarily or permanently lose access to certain features, like Direct Messages or Giveaways.
Discovery restricted:
If you’re a Seller, your visibility in Feeds and Recommendations is temporarily reduced. Your shows will still appear on your profile, in Search, and in the “Followed Hosts” feed.
Temporary account suspension (2 or 7 days):
You lose access to your account for a set period. When the suspension ends, your access is restored unless you’re notified otherwise.
Ongoing investigation suspension:
In certain cases, your account is suspended while a serious issue is under review. The suspension stays in place until the review is complete.
Selling suspension:
If you’re a seller, you temporarily lose the ability to sell or host shows. You can still access your Seller Hub, ship orders, respond to support requests, and use DMs during the suspension period. Your Account Health rates continue to update based on how you handle existing orders and buyer requests during the suspension. Once it ends, your ability to sell and host shows will be restored unless you’re notified otherwise.
Loss of selling access:
If you’re a seller, you permanently lose the ability to sell or host shows on Whatnot. Once you lose selling access, you cannot access any seller features.
Ban:
Your account is permanently banned. You can no longer buy or sell on Whatnot.
As set out in our
Community Guidelines
, to maintain the integrity of our platform, we do not allow users who have been banned, suspended, or restricted to use Whatnot with a different account, or otherwise circumvent our enforcement actions. Account action and removal may extend to related user accounts.
Do violations expire?
Most violations expire after
180 days.
Permanent penalties, like
bans or loss of selling access, don’t expire
.
How do I appeal a violation?
If you believe a penalty was applied in error, you can appeal by logging in to Whatnot and going to the
Policy Standing
section of your
Account Health dashboard
. There, you can review the violation and submit an appeal if you’re eligible.
After you submit an appeal, our team will review the relevant details and notify you of the outcome.
How can I check my policy standing?
Visit the
Account Activity
section in your
Account Health dashboard
to view your overall standing and any violations from the past 180 days.
Checking this regularly can help you stay in good standing, understand any issues on your account, and avoid future penalties.
